# Open questions, risks, and unverified claims

This file lists every open question, risk, and unverified claim in the design that an implementer must not assume. Each row gives the question in one sentence, where the design states it, what depends on it, and the work package in `build/BUILD-PLAN.md` that settles it. "No work package" means no gate row or planned step answers it, and the row says who can.

Evidence tags carry over from the source: [inference], [guess], [unverified], and [unknown], plus [measured] and [doc] where Milestone 0 has answered part of a row. Rows marked "found by the build plan" are sequencing questions that `build/BUILD-PLAN.md` raised while placing work. They are [inference] and not in the design. Rows marked "from a walk report" are questions that a writer of the implementability walk left open in `history/review/walk-fix-reports.md`. Rows marked "from a third-walk report" are questions that writers X1, X2, and Y of the third walk left open in `history/review/walk-rest-reports.md`.

Sources: "Open questions and risks" in `design/DESIGN.md` and `design/AGENT-LAYER.md`, every `[unverified]` tag in `design/`, the pending Cloudflare-design decisions (`decisions/DECISIONS.md`, "Four Cloudflare-design questions still carry the run's defaults", which copies `history/FRAME.md`, "Pending operator decisions for the Cloudflare design"), the flip conditions in `alternatives/AWS.md`, "When the AWS design would be the better choice", the flip conditions in `design/DESIGN.md` and `design/AGENT-LAYER.md`, "Synthesis decision", and the writer reports of the implementability walk (`history/review/walk-fix-reports.md`) and of the third walk (`history/review/walk-rest-reports.md`). The walk answered the build plan's earlier sequencing questions R4 to R8, and `design/DESIGN.md`, "Milestones 1 to 7", now places that work (`decisions/DECISIONS.md`, "Implementability walk").
