# F67: a group with requests waiting must elect (2026-11-12)

Branch `f67-liveness`, from `main` at `fd6e711`. F67 (`decisions/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`): repository and `Authz` groups
were left as three followers at high terms with no leader after load (B0-35 on `graft-m2b`, term 4 after 150,399
`/suspect` and 78,842 `/read`; LM-1 round 5's `Authz`, terms 53 and 55; B0-30 on `graft-m1`, three gaps on Nov 11),
and only the test-only `/campaign` restored them. This work states the liveness property, finds every path to a stuck
leaderless state in `crates/raft`'s simulator, fixes each in the core, and makes the sweep fail on the class.

## The property

While the same two or more voters stay up, linked to each other, and free of gray faults, and a client request is
waiting on the group, some request completes within T = 30 s. T is LEASE (9 s, a transfer onto a bad site holds
commits that long), plus the 2 x ELECTION_AFTER (20 s) waivers of RESET_QUARANTINE and the drain bar, plus about 1 s
for a campaign and a round. An idle group is never woken: campaigns stay request-driven
(`decisions/DECISIONS.md`, "The site-loss bound is per request").

The simulator's monitor (`crates/raft/tests/common/sim.rs`, `watch`) measures it directly: a stretch over which the
healthy set is fixed at two or more voters, a client op is outstanding, and none completes. A change in the healthy
set starts a new stretch, since the property assumes it holds. `sweep_with` asserts every seed's longest stretch is at
most `LIVE_BOUND_MS` (30,000).

## What the simulator gained

- `FaultKind::HalfHung(p)`: requests arrive and their answers are lost with probability p (B0-07's hung calls,
  `drop_append_reply`). Half-hung faults last 30 to 90 s, longer than any protocol timer, so a stuck state shows past
  the bound instead of ending with the fault; other gray faults last 5 to 30 s.
- Host moves: `transfer` (a `STEP_DOWN`-like `transfer(to)` to a random other voter) and `drain` (`set_drained` on a
  site for 2 to 20 s); `down_max_ms` for quick member resets (an isolate eviction, which drops in-memory timers).
- `Edge`: the hosted lookup. `follower_reads` sends a get by read-index to a follower first; one not answered within
  FOLLOWER_WAIT (1 s) sheds as a 503 with no `suspect`, and only `NotLeader` goes on to the leader path, which
  reports. `suspect_gap_ms` damps one client's same report (SUSPECT_GAP). `reads_only` models an `Authz` group under
  API load.
- `live_faults` mixes these by `seed % 4`, reads only on `seed % 8 >= 4`.

## The paths, each with a reproduction on the old core

Every regression test named here fails on the core at `fd6e711` and passes on `f67-liveness`.

1. **A half-hung leader holds its followers.** Its appends arrive and its followers' answers are lost, so they renew
   their promises every round, every `suspect` answers `Promised`, and the leader commits and grants nothing. A
   reads-only group never elected: 4 of 8 seeds were still leaderless after 60 s. Fix: check quorum by demand. A
   ready leader with an entry, read, or transfer waiting that no quorum has answered for ELECTION_AFTER stops leading
   and clears `led_term` (`leader.rs`, `tick_leader`; `heard` records AppendEntries answers, since a transfer's lease
   reset clears `acked_at`). An idle leader is never moved. Tests: `leadership.rs`
   `a_leader_no_quorum_answers_stops_so_the_followers_can_elect` (fails on the old core),
   `an_idle_leader_with_lost_answers_keeps_leading` (the control, passes on both); `liveness.rs`
   `a_read_only_group_elects_off_a_half_hung_leader` (seeds 0 to 11; old core seed 4: never; fixed worst about 20 s).
2. **Reads only through followers never trigger a campaign.** A follower read sheds as 503 with no report, so a group
   whose clients only read through followers and learners has no trigger at all. Fix: the design's "read-index call
   to the leader fails" is a batch unanswered for CAMPAIGN_DEADLINE (3 s). The follower forgets that leader, campaigns
   if its promise lapsed, and fails the reads `NotLeader` naming none, which the edge reports (`install.rs`,
   `check_remote`). Test: `leadership.rs` `an_unanswered_read_index_batch_forgets_the_leader_and_campaigns`.
3. **A transfer target that cannot win holds both voters.** A granted transfer vote promised the target for
   ELECTION_AFTER; a target whose answers were lost never led, and both voters refused everyone else as `Promised` for
   10 s (live kind 0 seed 30: 33.7 s). Fix: the promise runs CAMPAIGN_DEADLINE; a winner renews it with its first
   append (`election.rs`). Test: `leadership.rs` `a_transfer_vote_promises_the_target_only_for_its_campaign`.
4. **A quarantined campaigner contends.** A member inside its own RESET_QUARANTINE denied rivals as `Contending` while
   every voter denied it as `Quarantine`, until the waiver at 2 x ELECTION_AFTER (seed 7273: 25.3 s). Fix: only a
   member that `may_take_lead` contends. Test: `scenarios.rs` `a_quarantined_campaigner_does_not_contend`.
5. **A member refused as `Suspected` stands aside past the bar.** It answered every report `Busy` for 2 x SUSPECT's
   window (20 s), outlasting the voter's bar on it, while the other member was `LogBehind` (gray seed 10395: 31.6 s
   with fixes 1 to 4; 15.6 s on the old core, where check quorum had not yet moved the leader). Fix: it keeps
   campaigning by pre-vote, which moves no term, and only stops contending. Test: `sim.rs`
   `a_member_refused_as_suspected_keeps_campaigning` (fails with this one change reverted).
6. **A forgotten leader's reports stop counting.** Under fix 2 a follower forgot its half-hung leader but still
   promised it, and `suspect` counted only reports about `self.leader`, so two colos never withheld its promises
   (live seed 7337: 47.8 s with fixes 1 to 5). Fix: a report counts against the leader the follower promised whenever
   it names no other leader (`election.rs`, `on_suspect`).
7. **A withheld leader's appends block the waiver.** Two healthy voters that each quarantined the other waited for
   2 x ELECTION_AFTER without a leader seen, but the half-hung leader's appends kept resetting `last_leader_seen`
   (live seed 11089: 32.9 s). Fix: appends from a leader the follower withholds promises from do not count as seen
   (`follower.rs`). Test for 6 and 7: `sim.rs` `a_half_hung_leader_cannot_hold_its_followers` (seeds 7337, 11089).

Two candidates were tried and rejected: promising only after an entry of the leader's term committed (it broke
`flapping_member_is_quarantined`, `promise_bars_a_rival_until_it_lapses`, and `reads_need_the_lease_or_a_round`),
and letting a promise or suspicion bar yield to a newer log (unneeded once the rules above held). Check quorum by
progress (commits and grants) in place of answers heard changed no stuck seed and was reverted.

Suspect damping (`SUSPECT_GAP`) never suppressed the only trigger in any seed: every stuck stretch had reports
arriving and answered `Promised`, `Busy`, or `MayNotLead`. Storage stalls (`SlowConfirm`, `FailWrites`) moved leaders
by `STEP_DOWN` or election within the bound in every seed.

## The site host

No change. The host's ticker runs while a read or ticket waits (`Host::has_waiters`), so `check_remote` fires on a
follower whose batch is unanswered; a follower's read that fails `NotLeader` naming no leader is declined by
`read_spread`, which goes on to `call_opt`, and `call_opt` reports a voter that answered `NotLeader` with no leader to
every voter. A follower that forgot its leader names none, so the edge reports [inference from the code; the hosted
fault below is the check].

## Open: three followers at term 2 after setup

narrow-reads saw two fresh 500-PR repositories (repo:65557, repo:65558) left as three followers at term 2 right after
B0-35's PR-opening setup, while learner placement retried for about a minute; `/campaign` on site 0 answered
`resumed`. The hosted run below reproduced the shape on an idle group: a member that holds `led_term` restarts as a
follower, and only a request resumes it. Commands and reads call `lead_or_resume`; the test driver's `leaderOf` polls
`/status` and `/learners` refuses a follower, and neither resumes, so the placement retried for a minute with no
leader [inference from the code]. Production requests elect it; F67 stays open until LM-1's rows confirm it.
