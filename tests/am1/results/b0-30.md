## Blocked on (2026-11-10 02:00Z), and how each is routed around

1. **Member SQLite over 4 GiB cannot be read.** workers-rs 0.8.7's `SqlStorage::database_size()` is
   `self.inner.database_size() as usize`, and `usize` is 32 bits on wasm32, so every reading saturates at
   4,294,967,295 (`tests/m2/results/m2-wp1.md`, "Ceiling", saw the same). The probe of `repo:196627` read exactly that
   on all three members. B0-30's line is 8 GiB, so the gate quantity is unreadable through the binding. **Route:** the
   site reads `ctx.storage.sql.databaseSize` as an `f64` through `js_sys::Reflect` (test routes only: `/fold
   {"log"}` and `/bench_seed`), shipped in this branch's site day.
2. **Nothing counts `SQLITE_FULL`.** A failed storage write resets the member (`Host::fail`) and logs "storage write
   failed". **Route:** members count writes that fail with `SQLITE_FULL` or "database or disk is full" in `/stats`
   (`sqliteFull`, per isolate), and the monitor also queries Workers Logs of all four site scripts for both strings
   every hour, which survives isolate restarts.
3. **The existing 4 GB groups are not usable.** `repo:196628` (BF-20's 3.99 GB tier) had no leader at 01:14Z (sites 0
   and 1 did not answer `/status`, site 2 a follower at term 18), and its repository answers 503 "not leader".
   `repo:196627`'s members are already past the 4 GiB reading ceiling (about 6.6 GB by M2's per-row rate). Both are
   owned by the BF-20 seed accounts, not by `m3org`. **Route:** a fresh `m3org/b0-30` repository, seeded on all three
   members through `/bench_seed` (the comment-row seed BF-20 used; `{"load"}` is the push load, not the seed).
4. **Push rate.** One network validates about 2 pushes/s (`tests/am1/results/am1-runs.md`, "Diagnosis"), and journey
   9 needs 4.17/s. **Route:** as the brief allows, see "Substitutions" (to be filled in when the run starts).

### Update 2026-11-10 09:00Z: what happened to each route, and two more blockers

- Routes 1 to 3 are built and shipped (commits `7bb30e0`, `724f954`): `/bench_seed {"b030": {"size": true}}` reads
  `databaseSize` as an f64, `sqliteFull` counts space failures, and `m3org/b0-30` (`repo:1048586`, created through
  Authz `CreateRepo` because REST create needs 32.13, which graft-m1 does not enable) was seeded to 1,340,000 comment
  rows of 1,800 B on all three members: **4,017,184,768 B each** at the end of the seed (07:5xZ).
- Route 4 is no longer needed for validation: `origin/main` `1e25da8` (merged as `8d73e9c`) has `fanout`. But see 5.
5. **Every edge route that reads the repository's tiers dumps the 4 GB tier.** `workers/edge/src/api.rs`,
   `tier_rows`: "The repository's `{"rows": true}` read on its leader", which `workers/site/src/family.rs`, `read_json`,
   answers with "both tiers' rows at this index, at most `graftyard_api::hosted::ROWS_MAX`" (50,000). Its callers include
   agent and App pushes (`workers/edge/src/agents.rs`, `push_context`: `let rows = match
   crate::api::tier_rows(c, repo, None).await`), the task and lease API, and GraphQL and REST repository reads. On the
   4 GB tier one such `/read` used about 302 s of CPU and reset the leader (08:03Z and 08:37Z); the 4 GB members took
   about 5 min to answer again each time. This is a defect for any hot group, not only this row (left as found: no
   design or edge edits here). **Route:** no agent-class pushes and no live edge commands on the 4 GB group; pushes
   are m3-admin's (Human class: `push_context` returns before the read); agent rows are recorded by the leader in
   batched entries (`/bench_seed {"record"}`), see "Substitutions".
6. **The first archive took the leader's isolate down.** With a test cap (`/bench_seed {"archiveCap": {"max":
   100000000, "range": 2097152}}`) the leader archived 1..1 and 2..39 at 08:50Z, then logged "Cannot invoke closure
   from previous WASM instance" and the group sat leaderless at term 7 (applied 332/331/331). After an archive,
   `Host::truncate` calls `crate::load::set_source`, whose id-block check read **every** log row above the archive into
   memory (`SELECT row FROM log WHERE idx > ?`): 120 MB here, and up to `LOG_MAX_BYTES` (2 GiB) in the design. **Fix**
   (`c3a2aa9`, site code, test and production builds): the check reads only rows under 4 KB, since an `IdBlock` entry
   carries no events. Shipped by site day 5.

## Substitutions

Journey 9 as M0 replayed it (`tests/m0/results/m0-wp9.md`, B0-30) is 30 simulated days of 74.4 GB of inline agent
packs plus agent metadata rows. The hosted run keeps the bytes a simulated day and the metadata rows a simulated day,
and changes how they arrive (`tests/am1/b030/load.mjs`, header):

| What | Journey 9 (M0) | Here | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speed | 30 days | 30 simulated days in 40 h (18x) | the brief's 40 h |
| Pushes | 4.17/s agent pushes, 10 to 70 KB | 1.6/s Human pushes of 322,917 B to `refs/heads/b030/<i>` (500 branches), so the same 2.48 GB a simulated day (74.4 GB total) | 4.17/s x 18 = 75/s is not reachable; agent-class pushes hit blocker 5. 1.6/s stays under `hot`'s 2/s so packs stay inline. Each blob is a fixed base with 64 random bytes, so repacks delta them |
| Tasks and PRs | 6,050 a day via the task API | 6,050 a day recorded by the leader: `TaskCreated` (900 B summary), `PrOpened` (1.8 KB body) on a pushed branch, a 16 KB `PathSet`; `PrClosed` 20 simulated minutes later | blocker 5 |
| Statuses | 48,400 a day | 48,400 a day, 220 B description, recorded | blocker 5 |
| Provenance | 60,500 a day | 60,500 a day, recorded | blocker 5 |
| Batching | one command each | one entry of up to 40 tasks, 400 statuses, 500 provenance rows every 10 s | about 10 M separate commands would not fit 40 h |
| Request retention | 48 h | `GRAFTYARD_REPO_REQUEST_RETENTION_S=9600` (48 h / 18), repository chains only | at 48 h the `request` table would hold the whole run |

The metadata rows a day and the log bytes a day are journey 9's; the number of git pushes and the command count are
not. B0-30 measures storage, not push throughput.
