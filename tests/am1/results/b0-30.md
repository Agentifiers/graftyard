## Blocked on (2026-11-10 02:00Z), and how each is routed around

1. **Member SQLite over 4 GiB cannot be read.** workers-rs 0.8.7's `SqlStorage::database_size()` is
   `self.inner.database_size() as usize`, and `usize` is 32 bits on wasm32, so every reading saturates at
   4,294,967,295 (`tests/m2/results/m2-wp1.md`, "Ceiling", saw the same). The probe of `repo:196627` read exactly that
   on all three members. B0-30's line is 8 GiB, so the gate quantity is unreadable through the binding. **Route:** the
   site reads `ctx.storage.sql.databaseSize` as an `f64` through `js_sys::Reflect` (test routes only: `/fold
   {"log"}` and `/bench_seed`), shipped in this branch's site day.
2. **Nothing counts `SQLITE_FULL`.** A failed storage write resets the member (`Host::fail`) and logs "storage write
   failed". **Route:** members count writes that fail with `SQLITE_FULL` or "database or disk is full" in `/stats`
   (`sqliteFull`, per isolate), and the monitor also queries Workers Logs of all four site scripts for both strings
   every hour, which survives isolate restarts.
3. **The existing 4 GB groups are not usable.** `repo:196628` (BF-20's 3.99 GB tier) had no leader at 01:14Z (sites 0
   and 1 did not answer `/status`, site 2 a follower at term 18), and its repository answers 503 "not leader".
   `repo:196627`'s members are already past the 4 GiB reading ceiling (about 6.6 GB by M2's per-row rate). Both are
   owned by the BF-20 seed accounts, not by `m3org`. **Route:** a fresh `m3org/b0-30` repository, seeded on all three
   members through `/bench_seed` (the comment-row seed BF-20 used; `{"load"}` is the push load, not the seed).
4. **Push rate.** One network validates about 2 pushes/s (`tests/am1/results/am1-runs.md`, "Diagnosis"), and journey
   9 needs 4.17/s. **Route:** as the brief allows, see "Substitutions" (to be filled in when the run starts).
