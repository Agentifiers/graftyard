## Why the one clone in second took 5.2 s

[measured: `misslines-mt20b.jsonl`, `shardprocs-mt20b.jsonl`; times on Cloudflare's clock]

The clone started at 20:15:02.866Z, about 42 s into second and about 3 minutes after the home shard's kill (site a
was down at the 20:12:09 sample).
1. **Site a, the home:** the edge sent it first, with another site to try (`next=1`). The miss waited
   `CHAIN_WAIT_FORWARDED` for site a's chain step and answered 503 `warming` at 2,000 ms.
2. **Site b, from about +1.4 s:** `compose=only` answered 409, "stock: not a ref tip or behind HEAD". The shard
   caught up for 2,550 ms, then served the tail. Headers came at 2,568 ms after arrival, and the clone ended at
   5,195 ms.

- **The home's chain step was not ready for the whole second phase.** Site a answered 25 misses 503 at the 2 s chain
  wait, every one between 20:14:13 and 20:16:50Z; mt19 had 6. In that window site a was catching up after its
  restart, and its own geometric repack ran from 20:13:54 to 20:18:39. Site a is the network's builder and its only
  composed-chain stepper, so this is G37's question: should the chain keep stepping while the home's first shard
  rehydrates? [inference]
- **The shorter bound was not running at the miss.** Site b was held, not repacking, at 20:15:02 (unkept 351), and
  its overdue run started 67 s later. Site b's 2.55 s catch-up ran with maintenance held [inference from the 15 s
  samples].
- **The kill phase moved the tip twice as often as mt19's** (18 against 9; mt18 31). More catch-up was left after
  the kill, and second-phase misses were slower: p50 2,901 ms against mt19's 1,714 [measured]. mt19's 100% came on a
  lighter kill.

**So this row does not separate the shorter bound from the cause of the miss.** The every-clone miss and the kill
line's straddler both come from post-kill paths that maint-yield does not touch: the home's chain readiness, catch-up
after tip moves, and validate tries. The lead decides whether a second confirmation row is wanted, or a change on
G37's side first.
