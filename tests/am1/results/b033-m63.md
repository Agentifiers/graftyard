## Why the gate failed

**1. The leader change came from a `CommitErrors` step-down that step-agree let through.**
- Site a's alarms: `StepDown { CommitErrors { calls: [(1, 22, 3), (2, 22, 3)] }, to: None }` at 19:05:41 (held), then
  `[(1, 18, 3), (2, 18, 3)]`, `to: Some(SiteId(1))` at 19:06:18. The new leader b then held 8 more trips
  (7 `CommitErrors`, 1 `Confirm { p99_ms 1270, slow 6 of 14 }`) with `to: None`.
- step-agree holds a hot group's trip unless `site_sick` (never set: F105) or the group stopped committing, with `hot`
  meaning more than 2 committed pushes a second over 5 s (`HOT_PUSHES`, `HOT_WINDOW`). In the 5 s before 19:06:18
  only 36 agent pushes were acknowledged (7.2/s against 22.4/s before the first trip). That is the client side; the
  leader counts committed pushes by entry time, and pushes committed in bursts between AgentCmds cuts, 0 to 56 a
  second. So the group read as not hot at that instant, and the second trip moved the leader [inference: the fold's
  push count at 19:06:18 is not traced].
- Each trip is 3 failed of 18 to 22 calls per follower, the same shape as m61's "CommitErrors holds" (3 of 14–19).
  Those calls' failures are deadline misses while the leader's queue held up to 669 agent commands [inference].

**2. Human and MergeQueue commits wait behind the agent queue.**
- On m63, 356 of 402 cuts ended at `AgentCmds` (64 agent commands, `agent-cmds-entry`), with 283 to 863 agent
  commands left after a cut in every 30 s window.
- Commit p50 rose to 4,178 ms, almost all of it `queue:slot`: p50 3,219, p99 8,127, the wait for the entry in flight
  to apply. The round itself was p50 477, p99 1,444.
- With 9.6k fewer tier sheds, more agent commands reached the leader: 82.3/s against 69.2/s. The leader now cuts
  64-command entries back to back, one in flight, with humans in the same queue behind them.

**3. Agent throughput:** 1,559 of 12,498 agent pushes ok. The 10,938 full are admission 5,863, expire_agents 4,785, and
bound_at_send 290. Against m62 (bound_at_send 6,926), refusals moved from the edge's budget to the leader's queue: the
edge now admits faster than the leader drains.

## Largest remaining cause, and the next change

**The leader's single entry in flight, cut at 64 agent commands, is the limit.** It now sets both the Human line and
agent ok/s. F107's remaining whole-tier reads (GraphQL 407, issue labels 309) are 716 of the row's 1,993 503s, so
narrowing them is worth about 1.7% of writes and would not move the gate.

The ranked change: **a Human and MergeQueue lane that does not wait behind a full agent entry.** Either let a Human or
MergeQueue command cut its own entry early when the entry in flight is an AgentCmds cut, or reserve room in each cut
for those classes before `AGENT_CMDS_ENTRY` fills it.
- Expected effect [inference]: Human commit p99 falls toward one or two rounds (round p99 1,444 ms) instead of
  `queue:slot` p99 8.1 s.
- It does not raise agent ok/s. That needs rounds per second, not a larger cut, since AgentCmds already caps cut size
  for B0-32's stalls.

The step-down is a separate fix: step-agree's `hot` should not read a loaded group whose commits come in bursts as
cold, or a `CommitErrors` trip on a group that keeps committing should hold whatever `hot` says. That is the
"step-down fix" the re-base waits for.
