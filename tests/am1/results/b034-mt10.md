## Why kill and second composed on the shard

Proven from R2's own operation log for the packs buckets (GraphQL `r2OperationsAdaptiveGroups` by object name), the
clone-cache manifests in `graft-m1-packs-a`, and Workers Logs:

1. **18:33:53Z, a fold.** The builder (site a's first shard) folded the 20-pack chain on base `75afd029` into base
   `da3c45c7`: the last manifest naming the 20-pack chain is at 18:33:49 and the first naming `base-da3c45c7` at
   18:33:54. `ready_chain` (`crates/git/src/host.rs`) brings each warm bucket up in turn, own bucket first: the files
   the new head names, then `head`, then it deletes every file the new head does not name. Bucket a's `head` was put
   at 18:34:19. The last 200 for `base-75afd029…pack` in bucket a was at 18:33:48; from 18:34:45 every `get` of it and
   of its first three parts there is a 404. [unverified] R2's analytics list no delete call in bucket a in that
   minute; the 404s and the row-end listing (only `24fdd518` files) show the files went.
2. **18:34:39Z, the kill.** The home shard's container went away mid-step ("shard compose 1048603: Error: Network
   connection lost", then "not listening" at 18:34:42), before the step reached bucket b. Bucket b's `head` was not
   put again until 18:42, so bucket b kept the 20-pack chain on `75afd029` with all its files for eight minutes.
3. **Site b's shards served those misses.** They install the chain the first warm bucket names, their own first
   (`own_first`), so they held the `75afd029` chain and answered `tail=1` with manifests naming its packs (site b: 246
   `tail` answers 18:34:33–18:38:5x, `raw/misslines-mt10.jsonl`).
4. **The edge checked those packs only in the home bucket.** `manifest_answer` runs against `BUCKET_<home>` alone
   (`workers/edge/src/lib.rs`, the upload-pack miss path). Bucket a answered 404 for exactly the first
   `CHAIN_OPEN_AT_ONCE` = 4 packs of the old chain, 1,591 times from 18:34:45 through 18:38 (by minute: 146, 347,
   358, 397, 343), about 400 declined checks. The edge then asked again without `tail=1`, and the shard composed the
   whole pack: 246 of site b's 425 `composed` answers followed a site-b `tail` of the same key within 15 s.
5. **18:39–18:42:** no `tail` from site b and no compose `get` in bucket a, while site b answered 172 `composed`.
   [inference] The tail past the `75afd029` chain's tip grew past the container's `tailMax` (8 MiB): site b's chain
   could not move while the builder was down, and HEAD moved at one push a second. The tail's size is not logged.
   Site a's new container held no chain until the builder rebuilt base `24fdd518` at 18:42:13 (site a: 26 503s and
   one "stock: no chain" in 18:39–18:42).

So in kill and second a warm bucket held every pack the shard's manifest named, and the edge never looked there.
