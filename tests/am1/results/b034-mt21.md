## Site b's maintenance hold (G41)

[measured, `shardprocs-mt21.jsonl` (times +25 s), Workers Logs on `graft-m1-site-b`, a1's pushes;

|---|---|---|---|

**The hold began 4.7 minutes before the kill, on a healthy home.**
1. From the load's start the home was slow, not down. Its agent gate answered 52 agent validates 429 in the first
   25 s. Its human validates took 288 to 948 ms on the shard, and a human push's validate past 1 s hedged.
2. The first hedge reached site b at 03:10:59Z: `validate` with `wait=60&next=1`, a hedge's try. `hedged` calls
   `passed`, which marks the home cold in that isolate for `COLD_SHARD_MS` (30 s).
3. For those 30 s every push in that isolate tried site b first. `shard_url` gives try 0 `wait=15` whatever its
   site. The first such call reached site b at 03:11:04Z. Before the kill, 14 human pushes hedged, and 73 more
   validated on site b at their first try (a1's `validate_site` 1, `validate_tries` 1). Agents had 32 on site b.
4. `maint_hold` counts any `wait=15` client call on a non-home site as moved load. So site b held its maintenance
   from about 03:11:05Z. The holds went on until the last `wait=15` call at 03:19:38Z, refreshed at most every 10 s.

**The deferral bound held as built; the reading does not measure it.**
- **When the clock started.** `maintBegin` starts its clock at the first start it refuses. `startRepack` asks only
  past `repackAt` (50 unkept packs). Site b passed 50 at about 03:12:07Z.
- **Why it waited 7.0 minutes.** The overdue repack started at about 03:19:07Z, 7.0 minutes later. While client CPU
  p99 is over `fanoutUp` (0.75), `maintBegin` defers up to `maintDeferMax` plus `yieldHold`, 7 minutes. Site b's
  net-shard container ran 3.0 to 3.3 vCPU from 03:16Z (`instcpu.py`), so the CPU case likely applied. The
  container's `cpu_p99` was not sampled [inference].
- **What 573 s measures.** `longest_hold_s` is the span of continuous hold calls plus the last `yieldHold`: 03:11:05
  to 03:20:38Z. `maint.go` bounds deferral, not holds, and the hold went on through the overdue run. mt20b read 451 s
  for the same reason.
- **What 846 measures.** It is the unkept packs a hold call saw. Site b ingested from 3 to 886 unkept packs while its
  maintenance was held and then deferred. The repack at 03:19 to 03:20Z brought it to 139.

So the bug lead is upstream of the bound. **A hedge on a slow, live home turns a hedge's cold mark into moved load
for maint-yield.** That started site b's hold and its pack growth before any failover. `maint_hold`'s own doc says "a
slow but healthy home moves nothing" because a hedge's try does not count. The cold mark the hedge leaves sends the
next pushes' first tries to site b, and those do count. Not fixed or rerun, as ruled.
