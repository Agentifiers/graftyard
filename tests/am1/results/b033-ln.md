## Why the side writes were refused: the hosts, not the build

Ruled by the lead, 2026-11-24, on a $0 reading of today's raws. There is no code fix. Three findings carry it. h1A and
h2A, on one build, already span lnA. A native A/B of the guarded read path finds no difference. And lnA's learners
read 9.19 ms of CPU per request against clA's 5.8. The leader's per-call rise over the row is the row's own log and
fold growth, and it goes with F65 (`tests/am1/results/archive-memory.md`).

**Every side 429 is a member's shed, not the advert.** `read_repo` in `workers/edge/src/api.rs` (`shed_reply`,
`shed_for`) answers an Agent writer 429 `Retry-After: 1` when every member asked shed the tier-row read
("overloaded"). Over the first 300 s, edgefull's advert refusals are all pushes: h1A 826, h2A 499, h3A 896, clA 1,939
and lnA 1,079. None is a REST or GraphQL write. In the edge's 1% `apiTiming` sample, the tier read's p50 is 2,495 ms on
clA and 3,926 ms on lnA. The requests answered 429 have a tier p50 of 4,016 and 4,539 ms, which is a shed after
`SHED_RETRY_FOR`'s 2 s of retries.
