- **Clone-cache hits:** p50 606 ms, p99 2.8 s in j9 (n 43); p50 680 ms, p99 3.4 s in the second phase (n 34).
- **Composed misses by phase:** p99 10.3 s in j9 (224, all composed) and 18.0 s in kill (135, all composed).
- **The tail past 5 s is not the container.** It comes from:
  - stock misses that are over the diff cap or name a want that is not a ref tip, which hit the monorepo after the tip
    moves in the second and hostile phases;
  - 503s;
  - the hot networks' own probes. The hostile phase's p99 is the driver's 120 s abort: 21 of 90 aborted, and a
    hostile clone's p50 was 30 s.
- **The `to_shard` and `edge_net` columns mix two clocks.** `to_shard` p50 is 7.9 s while `edge_net` is −7.0 s on
  nearly every row: a constant offset of about 7 s between the orb's clock and the Workers Logs timestamps. The container
  and transit columns (shard-side differences) are unaffected. With the median offset (−7,041 ms) taken out, composed
  misses split as edge to shard p50 0.8 s, p99 7.6 s, and edge plus network p99 10.3 s (n 393) [inference: one constant
  offset per run]. So for composed misses, the wait before the shard object answers (`compose.ready()` and the
  catch-up) is the tail, not the container (p99 1.4 s).
