## The validate slowdown, bisected (2026-11-24, $0)

The lead asked what made `ok` agent pushes' `v_walk` on g4B's home grow from p50 63 to 576 ms when erDA's stayed 34
to 95, and whether G39's batched ingest or adopt-aside is the cause. **No change between 01471da2 and 5bed46af made a
validate dearer. The home took more of the validates:** 99% of agent validates answered on site a, against 76% on
erDA. The extra work made the gate refuse agents, and the per-validate time grew with how many ran at once. Opened as
G40, owned by F112.

**What changed in the validate path.** `git diff 01471da2 5bed46af -- containers/git` adds `/ingest-spooled-batch`
(G39's lever C) and shortens `MAINT_DEFER_MAX` to 6 min. Neither runs inside `/validate`, which takes the network's
lock shared, indexes the pack into a quarantine (`v_index`), then walks it: `rev-list --objects` against `--not`
every ref, `idxObjects`, and per update `rev-list`, `cat-file --batch`, `merge-base` and two diffs (`details`), up to
setting the pack aside (`v_walk`). `workers/edge/src/lib.rs` changed only `merge-front` and the fetch's `Retry-After`,
and `validate`, `shard_order`, `mark_shard` and `validate_hedge` are the same [doc].
