## The cold depth-1 hang

The same pattern as the first try's gate, in j9 and kill only:
- Probes at ≥ 100 s: j9 20, kill 5, second 0, hostile 0, all between 12:29 and 12:32, 1–4 minutes after t0 and just
  after the cold warm.
- Of 37 shard miss lines for 1048602 in 12:29–12:33, 34 answered `tail` with `ready_ms` 0, `caught_up_ms` and
  `first_ms` null; 3 composed. The shard logged `headers_ms` 271–750 ms: that is the shard's answer to the edge.
- Edge upload-pack invocations with wall time over 100 s: 23 (16 canceled with no status, 1 canceled after a 200, 6 ok
  with no status), each with about 9 ms of CPU.
- The hung samples are clone-cache hits (manifest entries) and composed misses: both go through the edge's
  `manifest_answer`, which opens every chain pack in R2 and holds each body open before any header leaves
  (`crates/git/src/host.rs`, `open_chain`).
- A scratch Worker probe (deployed, then deleted) shows the mechanism: in one invocation, with 8 R2 object bodies held
  open (first chunk read, the rest unread), a 9th `get` never resolves; it timed out at 15 s and at 170 s, opened in
  turn and 4 at a time. A manifest naming 9 or more packs therefore hangs `open_chain` [inference: the hung manifests'
  pack counts were not read before the networks were purged].

That is B0-34's cold line and the next code target (branch `b034-hang`).
