### The flip: did a catch-up wait over 1 s for the exclusive lock behind stock readers?

**No.** The exclusive section is `lock_ms` (from `l.Lock()` returning to unlock, `indexIngest`) and the wait for it is
part of `index_ms − lock_ms` (index-pack under the shared lock, then the wait for the exclusive one). Over the row's
280 stored-pack catch-ups (`raw/shardev-mt3.jsonl.xz`):
- `lock_ms − midx_ms` was at most 22 / 39 / 12 ms on sites a / b / c. Every exclusive section over 1 s (5.5, 4.9, 4.7,
  9.8, 7.6 s) is the midx write itself (`midx_ms` 5.5, 4.9, 4.7, 9.8, 7.6 s).
- `index_ms − lock_ms` per pack: p99 1.3 s with no stock fetch running on that container, p99 1.7 s with one or two,
  so stock readers add no measurable lock wait.
- The catch-ups whose ingest started late (`total_ms − index_ms` over 10 s: 4, at 14.5 to 23.7 s, all site a) are the
  `adopt` path in `handleIngest`, which takes the lock alone for a validated pack. That is a wait behind validates and
  ingests, not stock: two of the four had no stock fetch open, and stock was at most 2.

Option 4 stays held.

### The G26 finding: a chain with no `Settings` row still gets the first ref

The monorepo's `ranges` read answers `"default_branch": null` (read at 20:52Z): `b034setup.mjs` with `B034_CREATE=owner`
makes the repository by `CreateRepo` and `RepoOwner` only, so it has no `Settings` row, and `family.rs` answers null
"before the first `Settings`". The shard then sends no `head=`, and the container keeps the old rule, `refs/heads/main`
when it exists, else the page's first ref (`handleRefs`). So G26 did nothing for this network, and m1b34's failure
repeated:
- **Before site a's post-kill rehydrates ended (512 s):** 424 of 437 miss lines composed (97%), 0 over the cap.
- **After:** 1 of 25 composed; 15 lines named the cap (4 answered stock, 11 a 503 within the deadline). Site a's
  rehydrates ran 309 → 513 s, 440 → 632 s and 701 → 860 s, and the builder put a 20 KB base at 20:34:40Z (513 s),
  `base-e6f3565c…`, a commit in no linux history and not in the seed plan's 600 future tips: a row push, as in m1b34.
- **The edge gets it right:** its ls-refs takes HEAD from the same `settings` row and falls back to the shard when the
  row is missing (`workers/edge/src/lib.rs`, `ls_refs`). GHES's own reads take a missing row as `main`
  (`crates/git/src/transfer.rs`, `defaults`).

This is a gap in G26, not test residue: every repository made without REST create (imports, `B034_CREATE=owner`)
has no row until its first settings change. The fix belongs in the shard path: `ranges` should answer the default
`refs/heads/main` when there is no row, as `defaults` does. Proposed, not built (it changes G26's decision record).

### The hostile phase

From 600 s the monorepo's clone probes waited 120 s (53 of 71) and a human push timed out at 200 s (5 of 198). The
shard side (Workers Logs, `raw/attr-mt3.txt`):
- **Site a** was rehydrating the monorepo from 701 s to 860 s, its third rehydrate of the row (a 137 exit each time,
  `raw/instcpu-mt3.txt`); site b from 701 s to 890 s. While a site rehydrates, every upload-pack there answers 503
  `warming` within its 15 s or 60 s, so the edge walks a → b → c.
- **Site c** had no compose chain all row (`compose/1048596/` empty in bucket c at warm and at the end), so its misses
  went stock: 44 of 49 monorepo upload-packs there in the hostile phase never answered within the edge's wait.
- So the hostile-phase clone tail is: both chained sites rehydrating at once, and the third with no chain. The
  deadline worked as designed (each 503 on time) but there was no site left to answer. m1b34's hostile phase had one
  rehydrate (835 s) instead of two overlapping.

Why the containers exited 137 three times on site a and twice on b is not settled by this run: container memory peaked
at 10.0 GiB, not 12, and stock was at most 2. [inference: the exits line up with each rehydrate's start, and the
first one is the row's own kill at 300 s; the other two are not.]
