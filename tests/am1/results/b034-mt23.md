## Recommendation (G41 line 3)

**Reframe the 751 line; neither shorten `MAINT_DEFER_MAX` nor back `maint-yield` out on the warm site.** [inference
from the reads above; the lead rules]

- **Shortening cannot keep c under 751 at this rate without going under the failover floor.** At 2.62 packs a second
  from 161, c passed 751 at +225 s. The clock started at −76 s, so a bound that held c under 751 would be under about
  300 s. `maint-yield` set its floor at 327 s, the kill's 120 s plus a rehydrate's 207 s
  (`TestMaintDeferMaxFitsAFailoverAndThePackLine`). This row's rehydrate took 326 s, so the floor if anything grew.
  An earlier overdue repack would also land closer to the kill phase, the window the line guards.
- **Backing it out on the warm site removes it where it matters.** Under `warm-order` the warm site is the one that
  carries a failover. Every-clone passed at 100% and 96.4% with the hold in place, and the slow misses came after the
  hold ended in a repack, not during it.
- **The 751 count measures nothing this row could see.** It is `m2-wp6`'s slow-merge count, and B0-34 has no merges.
  Past 751 and before the repack, misses and validates on c stayed near the kill phase's. What hurt was the overdue
  repack at CPU p99 1.0 under the hostile phase's load on a site carrying all five networks.
- **The reframed line:** on the site that carries moved load, read client cost instead of pack count. For example,
  miss headers p99 and validate `v_walk` p99 while held, against mt17's warm site, with the 751 count read beside it.
  Add a merge row if the merge-cost clause is to stay testable.

**The open question the lead may want first** is what the overdue repack does to clients when it starts under full
load. On this row it is the 79 of 84 misses over 5 s, in a phase the every-clone line does not judge. That points at
`maintBegin`'s grant past the bound (it ignores client CPU) rather than at the deferral length. It also points at two
confounds a next row could remove: placement (choose a network set split across b and c, as mt17's was) and the late
rehydrate.
