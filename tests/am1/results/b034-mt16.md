## The hits and misses that opened no chain from records

Five second-phase hits (`chain_open` 488–972 ms, including the 1,551 ms hit) carry no `chain_head`, `chain_set` or
`chain_chk`. So do twelve composed misses: four at 09:09:26–31Z, four at 09:12:42–45Z and four at 09:15:39–41Z.

**Which path they took.** These are the declined chain path, by the code:
- `served` (`colo::ChainSets::served`) is called on every bucket that checks (`check_with_sets`, `crates/git/src/compose.rs`).
- `Late` answers 503 (`chain_late`), and the row has 0 such answers among them.
- So a 200 hit with `chain_open` timed and no `served` is a manifest that every chain bucket declined. `clone_hit` (`workers/edge/src/lib.rs`) then served the stock cached entry (`cached_response` on `r2_key(0)`).
- The misses' `x-graft-compose: composed`, not `composed: r2`, is the same decline on `manifest_answer`. The edge went back to the shard for the composed pack.

**Why the manifests were declined: a fold.**
- Each burst sits at a fold. The head's file count dropped from 20 to 1 at 09:09:32Z, to 0 at 09:12:46Z and to 1 at 09:15:43Z.
- A fold writes a new base and deletes the files the old head named.
- A manifest written against the old chain names packs that no bucket holds any more, so each bucket's check of them fails. A bucket holds them short, or not at all.
- The 488–972 ms is that work across up to three buckets: a head get, then checks that fail.
- [Inference] The timing pins the cause to the fold: every burst falls within about 6 s of one. I did not read the bucket listings at those instants.

**Should `head-etags` cover it? No.**
- `head-etags` tells the edge which bytes to trust for packs the head names. These manifests name packs the new head does not name, and that are gone.
- A head record cannot make them servable. The answer is correct as it is: the stock entry, or a composed miss.
- A cheaper decline is possible: when the head read names none of a manifest's packs, decline before checking them.
  - The fold deletes old files after the new head, so for a moment such a pack may still exist. Declining early loses only a composed answer whose fallback serves the same clone.
  - It would save the 488–972 ms on hits within seconds of a fold: 5 of 532 hits here.
  - Not built; it is an option, not needed for any line.
