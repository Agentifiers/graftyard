## Verdict

1. **j9 hits p99:**
   - Measured: **1,263 ms** over 193 hits, which fails the 1 s line (mt15: 2,190).
   - Outages removed: **713 ms** over 191 hits, which passes.
   - The four hits over 1.1 s are j9's first four, all within 103 ms of t0, at 1,160, 1,215, 1,263 and 1,355 ms.
   - After the first 1.1 s, p99 is 713 ms over 188 hits, with none over 1.1 s.
2. **First-second front p50:**
   - The first four j9 hits give **680 ms**, the median by the same definition as mt15's 1,178. Their fronts were 578, 937, 530 and 781.
   - That is a fall, but `beside` did not cause it: three of the four had `resolve_guess` 0, so they took no guess at all.
   - The flip as written ("does not fall") does not fire.
3. **Gate p50 with guesses against without:**
   - With `resolve_guess` 2 and `gate_beside` 1: **n = 0**.
   - Only one clone in the row had `resolve_guess` 2: j9's second probe. Its `beside_rows` read took 248 ms, but its gate was served from an isolate entry (`gate_iso` age 980 ms). So `gate_beside` never appeared in the row.
   - Without guesses: the cold `resolve_guess` 0 hits (n 4) had a gate p50 of 361 ms. Every gate came from a member read with no colo entry (`gate_match` 1–14 ms).
4. **`chain_open` p99 on a new head version:** **232 ms** on the 684 hits served from the head's records. The flip limit is 400 ms.
   - **`chain_head` share:** 98.5% of colo-arm opens (672 of 682) and 529 of 532 hits.
   - `chain_set` was 0 on every open: the head's records answered every pack, so no header set was used.
   - 18 opens checked one pack each (p99 456 ms).
   - Every open with a head record, hits and misses together: p99 380 ms.
   - Hit opens, all of them (`mt15v.py`): p99 263 ms. In mt15 the hits that checked 11 to 20 packs reached 469–1,209 ms.
5. **`beside_bad`: 0.** That is over one guessed read, which was not kept, so it says almost nothing about the mismatch rule.

**Flips:**
- `head-etags` stays: 232 ms on a new version is under 400 ms, and no clone was cut mid-answer.
- `beside`: neither flip fires, but the row did not exercise it.
  - The link entry is put only after a fresh `resolve` in that data center. A row's first requests reach a new monorepo (`b034-mono-g1`) on a new edge, so there is no entry to guess from. `b034-tail.md` predicted the same limit for warming.
  - After the first second, every isolate held its own guess (`resolve_guess` 1: 880 of 885 clones), and `beside` reads the link entry only when the isolate holds none.
  - The first-second front fell from 1,178 to 680 ms without guesses.
  - [Inference] The fall more likely comes from the sites' member reads, or from where the cold isolates landed. The samples don't separate these.
  - Whether `beside` helps a cold isolate with a link entry, and whether it is safe on a mismatch, is still unmeasured hosted.
