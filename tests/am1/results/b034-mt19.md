## Why site b was held from +67 s (read 2026-11-23, the lead's ruling after mt19)

- **The first hold call was a human push's `/validate` with `wait=15`, at + 63.0 s.** It is site b's first first-try
  validate of the row: 0 in the first minute. It answered 200 in 1,041 ms.
  - The shard object holds maintenance on such a call (`graftyard_git::maint_hold`: a client's `/validate`, first try,
    on a site other than the record's home 0).
  - `/procs` showed `held: true` at the next sample, + 67 s. All 69 first tries site b took before
    the kill were human validates. It took 0 agent validates and 0 upload-packs as a first try.
- **Why the edge's first try went past home.** Just before, from + 51.6 to + 62.0 s, site b served five human
  validates with `wait=60`. Those are later tries: hedges started after a human validate's home try ran 1 s
  unanswered (`VALIDATE_HEDGE`). a1's pushes show the same: three human pushes at + 59.8 to + 60.5 s
  validated on site c after 3 tries, hedged, at about 1.19 s each.
- **What site a was doing.** At the same time site a's shard answered agent validates in 3 to 5.4 s and turned
  agent validates away with 429 (full slots) at + 47 to + 69 s. Its human validates took 0.2 to 2.8 s.
- **How a hedge leads to a hold.** A hedge marks the passed shard cold in that isolate for `COLD_SHARD_MS` (30 s;
  `hedged`'s `passed`, `mark_shard`). The isolate's next push then puts the home last (`shard_order`), so its first
  try (`wait=15`) goes to site b: moved load by the rule.
- **So the hold came from a slow home, not a down or failing one.** That is the case `maint_hold`'s comment says does
  not count ("a slow but healthy home moves nothing"). That holds for the hedge's own try. It does not hold for the next
  push from the same isolate, which the cold mark sends elsewhere first.
- **Site b's first tries over time:** 22, 34, 8 and 5 a minute in the four minutes before the kill. After it, 242 to 328
  a minute while site a was down.
