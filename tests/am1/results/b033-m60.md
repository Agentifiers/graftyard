## 1. The Confirm transfers and site c's holds: load on the leader's links, not a follower's storage

The StepDown rule's confirm samples (`raw/stepdown-windows-m60.json`, the 22 alarms' windows) run p50 270 to 500 ms
in each trip window, with the slow ones at 500 to 1,544 ms. A sample is `confirm_begin` (at step, before the deferred
flush) to `confirm_end` on the member clock.

- **The leader's own storage was not the slow part.** The host's `sync()` (`Trace::Confirm`) on the same leaders: site
  a p50 24 ms, p99 217; b p50 15, p99 1,000; c p50 26, p99 1,140. Matching each slow rule sample to a `sync()` within
  400 ms: in 63 the sync was under half the sample, in 4 it was half or more, 6 had a sync over 500 ms, and 52 had none
  nearby. So most slow samples are the wait before or around the flush (the deferred flush, the turn queue), not the
  write [measured; the split's meaning is inference]. Leader stats agree: site a's `confirmSplit` gate p99 598 with
  sync p50 37–39, p99 89–179; b's sync p50 62–100, p99 1,000–1,166, turn p99 765.
- **Follower calls were slow on the wire and in the callee's queue.** Appends in each trip window: round trip p50 415
  to 1,000 ms, p99 1.4 to 5.9 s. The follower's handler p50 is about 0 to 325 ms; wire plus the follower's input queue
  (p50 315 to 934 ms) is most of it.
- **No one follower.** No call went unanswered. Calls over 1 s fall on both followers about equally, and each
  CommitErrors trip has 3 to 6 `CALL_DEADLINE` failures per follower, on both (`calls: [(0, 12, 3), (1, 12, 3)]` and
  so on). The leader took 1,300 to 2,600 `/commit` per 10 s inbound throughout.

So the trips are the leader and its links under load: a deferred flush waiting its turn, and appends queued behind
the leader's inbound commit traffic, after c (apac) led. That fits the far leader: both followers are trans-Pacific
from site c [inference].

**B0-02's graft-m3f gap does not match** ([B0-02 thread](https://ampcode.com/threads/T-01a1ccdd-804f-769d-b81a-5f937e3309df)).
There, site 0 went about 24 s without confirming because one `sync()` on its own storage took 23,957 ms
(`Trace::Confirm`), with no alarm, no failed calls, and the output gate held. Here no `sync()` passed 1,544 ms, and
the trips come from many slow appends and deferred flushes on both followers. One is a storage stall on the leader;

## 2. Agent throughput, 73.2 against 140 ok/s: the Agent token bucket at the cut binds

Agent pushes were offered at 41.6/s; 9.1/s were ok (m56 23.0/s). Side writes' ok rates were about m56's. Full
pushes per second by stage and minute: admission 11.0, 24.5, 20.1, 16.4, 12.9; expire_agents 11.8, 13.2, 10.8, 10.9,
7.5; edge (bound_at_send) 2.6 to 8.6.

- **What ends a cut** (`tests/m0/cuttrace/cuts.py`, 326 cuts in the window): AgentTokens 180, Empty 84, EntryMax 58,
  Soft 4. Tokens left at a cut p50 0. Agent commands taken 25,151 (83.8/s), with p50 110 and p99 735 left queued after
  a cut. m56: 524 cuts, 436 Empty and 63 AgentTokens, 27,394 taken (91.3/s).
- **The bucket's rate.** `refill` gives AGENT_SHARE (0.5) × capacity, and capacity is `(ENTRY_SOFT / cmd_bytes) ×
  1000 / round_ms` (`crates/raft/src/member/leader.rs`). At m60's 610 bytes a command an entry holds about 164, so the
  bucket gives about 82 agent commands per round; m60's AgentTokens cuts took 75 on average. By minute, the cut gap
  p50 went 441, 596, 559, 732, 781 ms, and agent commands taken per second 98, 90, 97, 81, 52.
- **Why the rounds were slow.** The mean gap between cuts was 852 ms (m56 546). 89 gaps were over 1 s and held 80 s
  past 1 s of the 275 s; 77 of those 89 began within 12 s before or during a StepDown alarm's window. So the holds and
  the far leader set the round, and the round sets the bucket [measured; the causal reading is inference].
- **admission and expire_agents are its queue.** A push is refused at admission when the agent queue ahead at the
  bucket rate, plus a round, outruns its budget (`expected_wait`), and expires when it waits in the lane past it. With
  110 to 735 queued and the bucket at 80 to 110 a second, both fire. The front left a median 2.5 s of the 5 s
  AGENT_PUSH_BOUND (p10 0.8 s), and pushes with under 1 s left were 14 ok of 2,893.

**What 140 would need.** At 164 commands an entry and AGENT_SHARE 0.5, 140 a second needs rounds of at most 586 ms
sustained: m56's near leader on site a reached it (gap p50 411, 135 agent commands taken a second in its second
minute), and m60 did not once c led. With m60's 852 ms rounds it needs AGENT_SHARE near 0.85; then the larger cuts take entries to the 1.5 MB ENTRY_MAX (AgentTokens cuts here already averaged
0.94 MB), so EntryMax would bind next. Neither is a code fix for this row: the first is placement and no holds, the
second a design change to a [guess] constant [inference].
