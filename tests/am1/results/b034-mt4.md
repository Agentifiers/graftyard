## The ranked fix candidate

**1. A catch-up's exclusive write waits behind validates holding the network lock shared [inference].** Both tails
come down to catch-up latency on the monorepo's busy first shard: 55 of the 56 slow clones waited for one, and the
slowest pushes waited for one (the 247-entry backlog, and b's at 481.6 s). `handleValidate` holds `lock(net)` shared
through `index-pack`, the connectivity walk and detail. `/ingest` and `/refs` take it exclusive, so a catch-up waits for
every running validate (8 slots on 4 vCPU, several seconds each under load). Go's `RWMutex` then holds new validates
behind the waiting writer, which matches the 70 slow validates that overlapped a catch-up and ended after it. On site a,
catch-ups whose longest in-flight validate had ≥ 3 s left spent p50 3.1 s of ingest outside the stored packs, against
1.0 s with < 1 s left (n 8 against 36). That is consistent, not proof: nothing measures the wait itself.

Code-only steps, in order:
1. **Measure:** `lock_wait_ms` beside `lock_ms` in the `/ingest` and `/refs` answers (and so in `shard catch-up
   stored`), as `midx_ms` was added. It confirms or kills the inference on the next row.
2. **If confirmed:** additive writers (adopt of new packs, ref updates) stop excluding validates; only deleters
   (`repack -d`, evict, init) still do. This is the validate counterpart of the held "stock off the network lock"
   option, with the same exposure to rule out (a validate reading a pack that `moveIn` is adding), so it goes as a
   design before code. Flip, on the same terms as the held one: the next row shows catch-ups waiting over 1 s for the
   exclusive lock behind validates.

Expected on the next row, if confirmed and built [guess]: `NOT_YET` waits near the catch-up's own ingest at idle
(p50 about 1 s), so the composed-miss p99 drops from about 10 s toward the no-`NOT_YET` 3.9 s; the kill recovery's 15 s
validates go too. Neither line passes, because of the medians above.

Ranked below it:
- A rehydrated shard keeps answering `warming` to `next=1` callers until its first tail catch-up ends. Certain from
  this row, but it touches only the kill's recovery: the top two human pushes and 2 of the validate 503s.
- k stepped 1 → 2 only at 259 s (`FANOUT_UP_FOR` 180 s [guess], a report each 60 s) while site a ran at 3.9 of 4 vCPU.
