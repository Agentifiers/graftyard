## The queue tail

Split from the leader's trace with `tests/fanout/slotsplit.py` (`tests/fanout/raw/tsave-m3fb29.jsonl.xz`, site 0, the load window). Method:
- Each `/commit` that reached the leader's handler (`Entered`, route code 3) is joined to the first cut at or after it. Every cut ended `Empty`, so a cut takes everything that arrived since the one before.
- Its batch is appended as the (held + 1)-th append after the cut.
- Queue is append minus arrival, split at the last apply as `/stats` does (`queue_split`).
- The trace covers 11,587 commands in the window against `/stats`' 10,521. Its slot and post (p50/p99 217/635 and 3/333) match `/stats`' 222/704 and 3/327 closely, so the per-command split stands for it [measured]. Arrival is the handler's entry, before body read and admission, so queue reads a few ms long [inference].

**By class.** The tail is not one class's. Mixed cuts are counted by each class's share of the cut:
- Human, weight 1,873: queue p99 739, slot p99 648.
- Agent, weight 9,714: queue p99 726, slot p99 628.
- Cuts of only one class: human (n 51) slot p99 361, agent (n 4,743) slot p99 554.
- No MergeQueue or Bot command was cut in the window.

**By frozen batches ahead at arrival:**

## The frozen-batch wait and the leader's CPU (analysis, $0, 2026-11-23)

The lead asked whether B0-29's frozen-batch wait is the same leader apply and transaction CPU that LM-1 found on B0-33
(`b033-pfa.md`, "What fills the leader's heavy turns"; `b033-pga.md`). If it is, LM-1's apply cut is B0-29's fix. If
not, the lead asked for a design for the rest. The three runs' traces have kind 25 `Entered` rows. The leader objects'
Workers Logs were still kept, so the join is exact. `tests/fanout/b029join.py` (new) prints everything below:

**Verdict: mostly not.** The leader runs the same kind of work as B0-33's, but little of it. Even with all of that
work removed, the replay leaves the median run over 1 s. LM-1's cut is part of B0-29's fix, not all of it. The rest is
the window between a batch's freeze and the in-flight entry's apply, and the round. A design for the window follows.

- **The round is not leader CPU either.** Near rounds (site 1) run p50 156 to 189 ms. The slowest 2% average 354 to

- **The window is the largest part.** Every cut ended `Empty`, so there is no backlog. The early cut freezes the next

### Design: re-ask a frozen batch's read until the entry before it applies (proposed, not built)

**Change**, in `crates/raft/src/member/leader.rs`: `cut_early`, `deps_read`, `decide_held` and `hold_batch`. These are
outside the functions LM-1 owns. While the entry before a frozen batch is in flight, and that batch was cut `Empty`,
each time the batch's dependency read answers the leader does two things:
- it moves every command admitted since the cut into the batch, within the cut's caps (`entry_soft`, ENTRY_MAX,
  AGENT_CMDS_ENTRY, the agent bucket);
- it asks one new `DepsWanted` for the batch's chains on the fold.

At `decide_held` the batch takes the newest read that answered. A command admitted after that read was asked waits
for the next entry, as today.

**Invariants.** One entry in flight is unchanged. `decide` is still pure on the fold at `applied`, the entry's earlier
commands and the deps: the newest read was asked after every command it decides was admitted, so read-after-admission
holds (BF-15, BF-27). This is `human-join`'s one-fold-per-chain rule, with one read out at a time, applied to a batch
that has no backlog and to every class.

**Why it is not `human-join` again.** That change was reversed on B0-33 chA and crA, where frozen batches waited 81 to
95% of wall time behind an agent backlog that refilled them. This change touches only `Empty` cuts, so a backlogged
batch (`AgentCmds`, `AgentTokens`, `Soft`, `EntryMax`) re-asks nothing and takes no one. B0-29's cuts were 100%
`Empty`. B0-33's were 24 to 53% `AgentCmds` or `AgentTokens` (pgA, sabA and pfA), so its `Empty` cuts would take the
change, and the flip below watches B0-33 too.

**Cost.** At most one extra dependency read per read time per group while an entry is in flight. At a 60 to 120 ms read
in a 100 ms window, that is about one more read an entry: about 4 to 5 a second on B0-29's leader (2,475 to 2,960 entries in 600 s), onto the owner's `Authz`
and `Tokens` leaders. A bigger entry is possible, but its bytes are capped, as today.

**Expected** [inference, the replay above]. D is about each run's own `deps:all` p50: 120, 58 and 115 ms. Mapped onto
`/stats` by each run's offset, the runs read about 958, 656 and 1,121 ms, a median of about 958. With LM-1's cut, whole,
they read about 869, 618 and 1,098, a median of about 869. That is under 880, so the median meets 800 at 90%, but
m3fb2r3 stays over the 1 s fail line. That run's slower near leg is placement, not something either lever moves (see
"Placement" above).

**Flip.** A hosted B0-29 row with the change whose dependency-read p99 (`deps:all`, or the owner `Authz` leader's
`/read` p99) rises by more than the commit p99 falls. Also a B0-33 row whose frozen-batch wall share rises over sabA's
69%, since the change should not touch B0-33.

**Order.** LM-1's cut merges first. Then one B0-29 row on graft-m4a measures it alone, against this replay's bound
(about 1,020 ms on the median run). Then this change is built and tested on the next three rows.
